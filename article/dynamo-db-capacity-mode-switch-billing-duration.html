DynamoDB Capacity Mode Switch Billing Duration

This article provides a concise overview of the billing mechanics and duration rules applied when switching an Amazon DynamoDB table between provisioned and on-demand capacity modes. It details whether a minimum billing duration exists, how charges are calculated during a mode transition, and the operational limitations that govern how frequently you can switch modes.

Minimum Billing Duration

Amazon DynamoDB has no minimum billing duration when switching between provisioned and on-demand capacity modes. You are not locked into paying for a fixed billing period (such as a full day or a full month) simply because you changed the table configuration. Billing adjustments take effect as soon as the transition completes.

How Billing Transitions Work

DynamoDB calculates your charges dynamically based on when the capacity mode change occurs:

  • Switching from Provisioned to On-Demand: You are billed for your provisioned Read Capacity Units (RCUs) and Write Capacity Units (WCUs) up to the exact moment the transition to on-demand mode finishes. Once active, provisioned capacity charges stop immediately, and you are billed strictly per read and write request unit consumed.
  • Switching from On-Demand to Provisioned: On-demand per-request billing stops as soon as the table switches to provisioned mode. From that point forward, you are billed on an hourly basis (prorated per second) for the RCUs and WCUs you allocate, regardless of actual consumption.

Switching Frequency Restrictions

Although there is no minimum billing duration, DynamoDB enforces operational limits on how often you can switch modes:

  • To On-Demand Mode: You can switch a table from provisioned mode to on-demand mode once every 24 hours.
  • To Provisioned Mode: You can switch a table from on-demand mode back to provisioned mode at any time without a 24-hour waiting period.

While the billing updates immediately upon completion of the switch, the 24-hour quota prevents continuous toggling back and forth between modes within a single day.
